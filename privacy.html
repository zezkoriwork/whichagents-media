Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

WhichAgents ("we") publishes AI news content on Instagram, YouTube and our newsletter. This policy explains how our publishing tool ("WhichAgents Publisher") handles data.

What the publishing tool accesses

  • Our own YouTube channel, only to upload our own videos (YouTube Data API, upload permission).
  • Our own Instagram account, only to publish our own posts.

What we do not do

  • We do not access, collect or store data about other users.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Google user data

WhichAgents Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access tokens are stored encrypted and used only to upload our own videos. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
